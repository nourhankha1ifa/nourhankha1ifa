# About Me

I'm a **Data Analyst & Data Scientist** from Egypt with a passion for transforming raw data into meaningful insights.

I believe every dataset has a story to tell, and my goal is to uncover those insights and present them in a way that supports smarter decision-making.

Currently, I'm looking for **Data Analyst**, **Business Intelligence**, and **Data Science** opportunities where I can apply my analytical skills while continuing to grow.

<br>

### [Amazon Product Analysis Dashboard](https://github.com/nourhankha1ifa/Data_Analysis/tree/main/DEPI/Power%20BI/Power%20BI%203)
